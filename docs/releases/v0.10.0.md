## EV Guest v0.10.0

How far the guest car has come – without the car in Home Assistant.

### Added
- **Estimated SoC** (`sensor.ev_guest_estimated_soc`): the guest car's estimated charge level while the plan runs. Without an energy meter it follows the plan (start level + charging time × charger power × efficiency, never above the target). With a meter it follows the energy actually delivered. It moves every minute while the car charges. Attributes: `source` (`plan`/`meter`), `start_soc`, `target_soc`, `planned_soc`, `planned_energy`.
- **Behind plan** (`binary_sensor.ev_guest_behind_plan`), only with an energy meter: on when the charger has delivered clearly less than planned – below 80 % of the planned energy and at least 1 kWh short, after 20 minutes of charging. For example a car that charges slower than the set power or stops by itself before the target.
- The panel's battery shows the measured charge level when there is a meter, and the status line warns when the car is behind its plan.

### Notes
- An energy meter stays optional. Without one, everything works as before and the estimate follows the plan.
- The estimate uses the inputs the plan was made with, so changing the inputs afterwards does not move it. Plans made with 0.8.0 or earlier have no estimate.
- Some chargers report their energy meter only now and then. *Behind plan* can then turn on for a while until the next reading.

### Rollback
The config entry version is unchanged (7.1), so downgrading to 0.9.0 works. The two new entities become unavailable.
