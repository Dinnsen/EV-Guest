## EV Guest v0.10.0

Your charger follows the plan – any charger – and is always handed back when the guest has gone. And you see how far the guest car has come.

### Added
- **Charger control** (`sensor.ev_guest_charger_control`): `released`, `charge` or `pause` – what your charger should do. EV Guest only holds the charger during a **guest session**, and a session always ends by itself: when the plan ends, on **New guest**, when a new calculation fails, or when the car has been unplugged for a minute. So a charger automation never blocks your own car because EV Guest thinks a guest is still there. Attributes: `session_ends`, `released_at`, `release_reason`, `car_connected`.
- **Car connected sensor** (optional, in the options): the charger's "car connected" binary sensor. It ends the guest session when the car is unplugged, and keeps a guest car that is still plugged in after the plan paused until it is unplugged – at most 24 hours – so it does not charge on at full price.
- **Blueprint** *EV Guest – charger follows the plan* for any charger: choose what starts and what pauses charging (a switch, a button, `monta.start_charging`, a current limit …). It pauses again when a car is plugged in outside a charging period, hands the charger back only after holding it, and catches up after a restart. Import it from the README.
- **Estimated SoC** (`sensor.ev_guest_estimated_soc`): the guest car's estimated charge level while the plan runs. Without an energy meter it follows the plan (start level + charging time × charger power × efficiency, never above the target). With a meter it follows the energy actually delivered. It moves every minute while the car charges. Attributes: `source` (`plan`/`meter`), `start_soc`, `target_soc`, `planned_soc`, `planned_energy`.
- **Behind plan** (`binary_sensor.ev_guest_behind_plan`), only with an energy meter: on when the charger has delivered clearly less than planned – below 80 % of the planned energy and at least 1 kWh short, after 20 minutes of charging. For example a car that charges slower than the set power or stops by itself before the target.
- The panel's battery shows the measured charge level when there is a meter, and the status line warns when the car is behind its plan.

### Notes
- `binary_sensor.ev_guest_charge_now` still follows the planned periods (and now turns off when the guest's car is unplugged), but don't switch a charger on it alone: it is also off when there is no guest. Use *Charger control* or the blueprint. If you use the automation from earlier READMEs, replace it with the blueprint.
- When the car is unplugged, the energy meter stops counting for the guest and the plan goes into the history up to that moment.
- An energy meter stays optional. Without one, everything works as before and the estimate follows the plan.
- The estimate uses the inputs the plan was made with, so changing the inputs afterwards does not move it. Plans made with 0.8.0 or earlier have no estimate.
- Some chargers report their energy meter only now and then. *Behind plan* can then turn on for a while until the next reading.

### Rollback
The config entry version is unchanged (7.1), so downgrading to 0.9.0 works. The new entities become unavailable, and an automation made from the blueprint then does nothing.
